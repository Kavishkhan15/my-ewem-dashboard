Read this correctly

Different LEED systems measure different things.

LEED BD+C focuses on building design and construction. LEED O+M evaluates the ongoing performance and operation of an existing building. A project’s certification level can therefore be recent while its rating system is an earlier LEED version.

01

Platinum and Gold are the achieved LEED certification tiers shown in the public project records.

02

v4, v4.1 and v5 are kept explicit so projects are not incorrectly compared as though they used identical criteria.
