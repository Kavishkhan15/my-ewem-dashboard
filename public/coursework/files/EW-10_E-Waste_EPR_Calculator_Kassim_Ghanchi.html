Basis of calculation

EPR target (Step 2) is drawn from the phase-wise obligation schedule in the CPCB FAQ: 60% of annual generation for FY 2023–24 and 2024–25, 70% for FY 2025–26 and 2026–27, and 80% from FY 2027–28 onward — this reflects Schedule-III/IV of the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022.

Registration fee (Step 3) is looked up from Annexure-I of the FAQ document, which bands the processing fee by the producer's annual e-waste recycling target: below 50 MT (₹2,500), 50–<100 MT (₹7,500), 100–<1000 MT (₹1,50,000), 1000–<5000 MT (₹10,00,000), and above 5000 MT (₹15,00,000).

Compliance status (Step 4) is a direct comparison required under Rule 4's EPR obligation: a company is "Compliant" only if its actual recycled quantity for the year meets or exceeds the calculated target; otherwise it is flagged "Non-Compliant."

Entity type (Manufacturer / Producer / Recycler / Refurbisher) reflects the four categories required to register on the E-Waste EPR Portal under Rule 4 of the Rules.
