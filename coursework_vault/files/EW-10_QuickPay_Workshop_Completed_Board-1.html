Facilitator tips (as run)

  • Timekeeper kept the countdown pinned to the top of the board throughout — no block was allowed to silently overrun.
  • Rapid Design ran long by 90 seconds; discussion was cut, not the clock, and the gallery walk absorbed the overflow.
  • "Ugly and fast" held — none of the 3 wireframes were polished, which is what let all 3 get sketched in 7 minutes.
  • Board was duplicated and saved as "Workshop Template – Research to Roadmap" immediately after the session for reuse.
